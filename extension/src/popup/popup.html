Link your browser

To seamlessly sync passwords, you need to pair this browser to your phone's JanusVault.

Option 1: Pair over Wi-Fi / Hotspot

Enter your phone's network IP (shown on its Sync screen, e.g. 192.168.1.15:51827) to connect.

Option 2: Pair via Bluetooth

Ensure JanusVault is open on your phone with Bluetooth sync enabled.

Option 3: Paste Code

Alternatively, open Trusted devices → Pair new device on the phone, and paste the code here.

Phone connection

Every reveal, autofill, and copy asks your phone to approve with its own fingerprint/PIN — there is no local fallback. Enter your phone's network address (shown on the phone's Sync screen), and make sure "Make this device visible for sync" is on there.
